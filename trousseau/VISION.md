# TROUSSEAU
### A keepsake in paper & petal — by Studio Vesper

> *trousseau (n.) — the collection of fine things assembled for a marriage.*

Trousseau is an anniversary keepsake told as a **wedding-stationery suite**:
as if a couple commissioned a fine-stationery studio to print their whole
relationship — one piece per year — and this page is the bright table where
the suite is laid out among roses, eucalyptus, and loose petals.

Third in the Studio Vesper series (after Perihelion, the sky, and Kept, the
drawer), and its opposite pole: **daylight, abundance, blush and sage.**

---

## The world

Warm ivory linen in soft daylight. Bone and white papers with deckled edges
and hairline gold borders. Painterly botanicals drawn entirely in code —
layered translucent roses, eucalyptus stems, baby's breath, loose petals,
soft watercolor washes — so that nothing ever sits alone: every card is a
**layered cluster** with florals crossing its corners, and every layer
scrolls at its own speed (parallax), giving each composition the depth of a
photographed flat-lay.

Palette: ivory `#f4eee1`, paper white `#fdfbf4`, blush `#d8a8a0`/`#c98d85`,
sage `#8f9271`, sepia ink `#5d4f3e`, muted gold `#ab8c58`, rose wax.
Type: Pinyon Script (names, signatures), Cormorant Garamond small caps
(labels, invitations), Special Elite (typewritten menus, postcards, notes) —
all embedded as data URIs; the file is fully self-contained.

## The first five seconds

No mechanic, no darkness: the names bloom in at full calligraphic scale over
a spread of roses and eucalyptus, like the cover of a stationery sample book.
*Ten years · 2015–2025 · a decade, in paper & petal.* Then: **scroll, slowly.**

## The arc

1. **Hero** — names in a garden. *"Some couples get an album. You get a
   trousseau."*
2. **I · Before** — two people already collecting the paper of a life.
3. **II · The Day** — the save-the-date, issued retroactively: October 14,
   2015, a bookshop, heavy rain.
4. **III · The Small Years** — the suite, piece by piece, one per year:
   a café **menu** (*two coffees, one gone cold — 2.40 / one conversation,
   four hours — gratis*), a **postcard** from the wrong train, a
   **change-of-address card** for the crooked floor, a **programme** for
   The Kitchen Dances (*nightly · from eight · dress: socks*), a
   **pressed-rose card** from the day of the yes, the **invitation** with a
   painted envelope liner and wax seal, and an **RSVP** from Biscuit the dog
   (*☑ accepts with pleasure / ☐ declines to behave*).
5. **IV · The year that didn't print well** — the light cools (never darkens),
   two dried stems, a plain card: *no cards were printed this year. two cups
   every morning, anyway.* *They kept it anyway.*
6. **The quiet** — a blank place card. *Some years needed no stationery at
   all. The best ones, usually.*
7. **V · The Full Suite** — every piece and flower flies in and settles into
   one abundant flat-lay: *"Not a monument. A table set with small proof."*
   Names, a letter signed *— the years*, and then blush **petals begin to
   fall** across the screen.
8. **Coda** — *set the table again.*

## Interaction rules

House grammar: scroll is time and reversible; scenes smoothed for weight;
parallax gives every cluster physical depth; the only UI is the chapter reel
and an opt-in generated soundtrack (a bright, warm chord). Petals fall only
at the end, in real time — the single ambient reward. `prefers-reduced-motion`
collapses everything to a still, readable spread.

## Template

One `STORY` object: names, dates, dedication, and seven memories whose `kind`
(menu / postcard / moved / programme / pressed / invite / rsvp) chooses the
stationery form, with `art` fields for the text printed on each piece.
