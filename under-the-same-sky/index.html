The night we met

Ten years ago the city was loud, the party was dull, and the fire escape was taken — by you. You pointed at the one star strong enough to outshine the streetlights and told me it was probably a satellite. It wasn't. I checked later. I checked everything about that night later.

A letter, by starlight

My love — people say the stars we see are old light, that everything up there already happened long ago. Maybe that's why the sky reminds me of you: the best things in my life already happened, and somehow they're still happening, every single day.

Ten years of you. Of fire escapes and kitchens, of ordinary Tuesdays that turned out to be the whole point. I don't wish on stars anymore. I just find one, and thank it.
