# KEPT
### A keepsake on paper — by Studio Vesper

> *kept (v.) — past tense of keep: to hold on to; to not throw away.*

Kept is Studio Vesper's second anniversary keepsake. Where **Perihelion** told
a love story in light across a night sky, Kept tells one in paper across a
writing desk: the conceit is that every love story leaves a paper trail —
receipts, tickets, notes left on kitchen tables — and this page is the drawer
where ten years of it survived.

---

## The first five seconds

A sealed envelope on a dark desk, lit like late afternoon. Calligraphy:
*June & Theo — on the occasion of ten years.* One oxblood wax seal with their
monogram. Then the cue: **"scroll, slowly."**

Scrolling *breaks the seal.* The wax cracks in half, the flap lifts, and the
letter slides up out of the envelope and unfolds to fill the screen. The
recipient physically opens their gift before the story says a word.

## The world

**Analog archival romance.** Everything on the page is a thing you could hold:
handmade paper with deckled edges (SVG displacement, no images), a bookshop
receipt with a zig-zag tear, a train ticket with punched holes, a café napkin
with a coffee ring, a torn notebook page with hand-drawn dance steps, a
pressed sprig taped under tissue, a letterpress date card, a wedding
invitation with hairline gold rules, a dog-license certificate with a rubber
stamp, a water-stained letter with the ink blurred where the hard years
soaked through. Chapters are typed; names are inked in script.

## Voice & type

Three voices, exactly like real stationery:
- **Special Elite** (typewriter) — the narrator's intimate, slightly worn voice
- **Pinyon Script** — names, signatures, chapter titles: the human hand
- **Cormorant Garamond small caps** — labels, years, the archival register

All three faces are embedded in the file as data URIs. The keepsake is one
self-contained HTML file with zero external requests — a locket made of code.

## Palette

Bone and ecru paper (#f1e9d9 → #ddcdae) on a deep sepia-oxblood desk
(#241310), sepia ink (#453425), muted gold (#a57d43), oxblood wax (#6d2b26),
olive botanicals (#6f6b48). Warm lamp-light pool, film grain, soft vignette.
The desk darkens for the Weather chapter and the silence — light is the score.

## The arc

1. **The envelope** — the seal breaks; the covering letter addresses *you two*.
2. **I · Before** — a library card and a lone cinema stub: two paper trails,
   nine miles apart.
3. **II · The day it started** — the letterpress date, and the bookshop
   receipt he kept and couldn't say why. (*He could say why.*)
4. **III · The slow dance** — seven artifacts, one per memory, each editable
   in the STORY object with a `kind` that picks its physical form.
5. **IV · Weather** — the water-stained letter, ink blurred. *Some pages
   warped. None were thrown away.*
6. **The silence** — a blank deckled sheet. *Some of it was never written
   down. The best of it, mostly.*
7. **V · Everything, together** — every artifact you passed flies into a
   thin gold frame and settles into one flat-lay: *not a monument — a drawer
   full of small proof.* Names, years, a closing letter signed "— the years."
8. **The seal** — a fresh wax seal presses the word *kept* shut.
   *Ten years · and counting.* Then: *open it again.*

## Interaction rules

Identical grammar to Perihelion: scroll is time and time is reversible (the
seal un-cracks if you scroll up); scenes are smoothed for cinematic weight;
no UI except the chapter reel and an opt-in generated soundtrack (a low warm
chord; a paper-crack when the seal breaks; a felt thump when the final seal
lands); `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything to a still, readable
desk.
