Chapter Three

The Moments That Made Us

The little moments that meant the most.

Our holiday trip

The first holiday you spent with my family. Watching you fit right in with them made me love you even more.

That time we got stuck in the elevator

Twenty minutes stuck between floors, and somehow we couldn’t stop laughing. That’s when I knew we’d be okay through anything.

Our first break up

We both said things we didn’t mean, and I hated every second we were apart. Finding our way back to each other is when I knew this was really it.

The big moments made the photos.

But it’s the small ones I’d keep —

slow morning coffees,

your humming in the kitchen,

doing nothing, together.

That was the whole of it.

A letter, for you

Hey Mar,

I don’t really do this, so bear with me.

I was thinking the other day about how much of my life has you in it now. Like I can’t remember what I did with my evenings before. That used to scare me a little. It doesn’t anymore.

You make everything less heavy. Even the boring stuff — grocery runs, sitting in traffic, doing absolutely nothing on a Sunday. I’d pick doing nothing with you over doing anything with anyone else.

I know I don’t always say things right, or say them at all. But I notice everything. The way you check on me when I go quiet. How you remember tiny things I mentioned once. I don’t take any of it for granted, even when it looks like I do.

That’s it really. I love you. I’m lucky. Don’t tell anyone I wrote this.

James
