## Full description

You know the tradition: a bundle of "open when…" letters, for the
moments you can't be there. Open when you miss me. Open when you can't
sleep. Open when we've had a fight.

This is that bundle — as a single beautiful web page they can carry in
their pocket. Six kraft envelopes with red-and-blue air-mail borders,
postmarks, and string-and-button ties. Tap one: the string unties, the
envelope unfolds, and there's your letter on ruled paper — maybe with a
photograph under a paperclip.

And the part paper can't do:

⏳ ANY LETTER CAN BE SEALED UNTIL A DATE YOU CHOOSE. The envelope shows
a live countdown ("opens in 342 days"), stamps itself "Sealed until…",
and gently refuses to open early. On the day, it unlocks by itself.
Perfect for an anniversary, a birthday, a homecoming.

WHAT YOU GET
• One single file (index.html) — the whole bundle, ready to open
• Six pre-written letters to start from (rewrite every word)
• A simple step-by-step guide

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the file in any web browser
• Tap "Write" and type your own words — labels, letters, P.S. lines, signatures
• Tap a photo frame inside a letter to add your pictures
• Set lock dates with one tiny, clearly-marked setting
• Tap "Gift" to see the finished bundle; "Reset" undoes everything
